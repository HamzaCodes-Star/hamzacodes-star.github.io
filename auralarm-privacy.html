2. Information We Collect

Auralarm operates primarily on a local-first principle. We minimize data collection to only what is strictly necessary to reliably wake you up and run habit tracking:

  • Account Information: Auralarm does not require an email or account sign-up. Each installation is assigned an anonymous Firebase Authentication UID to allow device-level synchronization of user preferences, habits, and alarms. This anonymous identifier is never linked to your personal name, phone number, or email address.
  • User-Provided Data: User-configured alarm times, custom labels, snooze intervals, wake-up challenges (e.g. math puzzles, shake sensitivity, steps), habit completions, and evening mood check-in notes.
  • Device & Usage Data: Technical device characteristics including device model, operating system version, app version, battery optimization state, and diagnostic crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics to monitor and improve alarm reliability across diverse hardware manufacturers.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Anonymous advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID) processed by Google AdMob to deliver advertisements.

3. Device Permissions & System Capabilities

To ensure alarms ring with 100% precision even when your screen is locked or the device is asleep, Auralarm requests specific operating system permissions:

  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM): Essential for triggering alarms at the exact millisecond configured by the user, bypassing Android Doze mode.
  • Full-Screen Intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Required to immediately display the alarm dismiss/snooze screen over the lock screen when an alarm fires.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Required to play continuous, uninterrupted audio tones until dismissed or snoozed.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used for upcoming alarm alerts, bedtime habit reminders, and persistent ringing notifications.
  • Physical Sensors & Accelerometer: Used locally in memory for shake-to-dismiss and step challenges. Sensor data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted or stored.

4. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing

Auralarm offers an optional evening mood check-in feature that provides customized morning wake tones using Groq Cloud LLM inference:

  • When you choose to complete a mood check-in, the text you submit is sent securely over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) to Groq Cloud for sentiment analysis and tone selection.
  • No Model Training: Your input data is strictly ephemeral, processed in real time, and is never used by us or our AI provider to train machine learning models.
  • Opt-Out: This feature is completely optional. Users may choose not to use mood check-ins without affecting the alarm clock functionality.
