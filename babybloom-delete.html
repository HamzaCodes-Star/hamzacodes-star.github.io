🛡️ Your Right to Data Erasure

At BabyBloom (developed by DuoDesire Tech), we believe parents own 100% of their family's data. You have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal and infant health tracking information at any time.

📲 Method 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Instant)

You can execute an immediate and permanent deletion of your account from within BabyBloom:

  1. Open the BabyBloom application on your mobile device.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Navigate to the Profile / Settings screen from the bottom navigation bar.
  4. Scroll down to Account & Security.
  5. Tap Delete Account and confirm your request.

Upon confirmation, your authentication session is terminated, and all profile data is immediately purged from our active databases.

✉️ Method 2: Request Deletion via Email (Web Request)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can submit a manual deletion request directly to our official developer support team:

Send your deletion request to:

duodesireofficial@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address registered with your BabyBloom account so we can verify ownership.

📊 Data Retention & Erasure Scope

When an account is deleted, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Account Credentials: Your user ID, display name, and email address in Firebase Authentication.
  • Baby Profiles: Baby names, birth dates, gender, and avatar photos in Cloud Firestore & Firebase Storage.
  • Care & Health Logs: All diaper logs, nursing & bottle feeding records, sleep sessions, medication records, and milestone notes.
  • AI Insights History: Generated summaries and pediatric guidance records.

Data Retained: We retain zero personal data after account deletion. Non-identifiable aggregate crash reports (without user identifiers) may remain in Google Play Console crash logs as standard technical diagnostic data.

Timeline: In-app deletions take effect instantly. Email-requested deletions are processed and fully wiped within 30 days.
