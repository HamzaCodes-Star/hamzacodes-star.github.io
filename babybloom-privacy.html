Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Official Legal Notice · Effective Date: September 30, 2026

👶 1. Commitment to Family Privacy

BabyBloom ("we", "our", or "the App") is built with a deep commitment to family and child data privacy. We understand the sensitivity of infant care logs and developmental records. We operate on a strict data-minimization principle: we only collect the minimum information required to log, compute circadian sleep predictions, and synchronize care records between authorized parents and caregivers.

Key Promise: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal family data to advertisers or third-party data brokers. Zero advertising SDKs are embedded in the App.

📊 2. Information We Collect

When you use BabyBloom, we process the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials managed securely via Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In or Email/Password).
  • Infant Care Logs: Timestamps and quantities for feeding (nursing timer, bottle volume), diaper changes (wet, dirty), sleep sessions (naps, night sleep), tummy time, medications (e.g. Vitamin D), and milestone achievements.
  • Pediatric Growth Metrics: Weight, height/length, and head circumference entries used to calculate standardized World Health Organization (WHO) growth percentile curves.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Anonymous crash reports, device model, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens required to deliver real-time caregiver sync and nap reminders.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To power interactive developmental timelines, circadian nap predictors (SweetSpot™), and WHO percentile growth charts.
  • To synchronize care events in real time across invited family members (parents, grandparents, nannies).
  • To generate optional smart pediatric digests using Google Gemini AI models. AI prompts receive strictly anonymized care parameters and are never used to train public foundational AI models.
  • To back up your family's records securely in cloud storage so you never lose milestones when switching devices.

👨‍👩‍👧 4. Multi-Caregiver Sharing & Access Control

BabyBloom allows primary parents to invite secondary caregivers to collaborate. All shared logs are visible only to users you explicitly invite. Primary account holders have full authority to revoke caregiver access at any time, terminating immediate sync to that device.

🛡️ 5. Security & Encryption Standards

All data transmitted between the BabyBloom mobile application and backend services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Database records in Google Cloud Firestore are encrypted at rest using AES-256 cryptographic standards.

🚸 6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

BabyBloom is a parental utility application designed for use by parents, legal guardians, and adult caregivers aged 18 and older. The application is not directed to children under 13, and children do not create accounts or interact with the software directly. All data regarding infants is inputted solely by their lawful adult guardians.

🗑️ 7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You maintain complete sovereignty over your information:

  • You can delete individual care logs or baby profiles directly within the App settings at any time.
  • You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated cloud records via Settings → Account → Delete Account, or by emailing our Data Protection Officer at support@babybloom.app. Deletion is executed within 30 days.
